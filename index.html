About me

Beyond The Code

I never followed a straight path.I followed curiosity.

My journey with technology didn't begin with a big project or a perfect plan.It started with a simple desire to understand how things worked.That curiosity led me to choose Computer Science and eventually step into anet centre cum studio, where I experienced technology from a differentperspective — helping people solve everyday digital problems and understandingthat technology becomes meaningful when it helps someone.

While pursuing my degree, I realized that learning was not limited to a classroom.I found lessons in people, places, conversations, the internet, and everyexperience I stepped into.

Design became one of those pieces.What started with creating logos, posters, banners, and visual contentusing Canva slowly introduced me to UI/UX and the world of designingexperiences.Later, my curiosity pushed me further — from designing what people seeto building what people use.

Today, I explore the connection between design and development as aPython Django Full Stack DeveloperandUI Designer.I enjoy creating digital experiences that are functional,simple, and meaningful.

I am still learning, still experimenting, and still discoveringnew possibilities with every project.

I don't believe every journey needs a fixed destination.Sometimes, the most meaningful paths are the ones that continuechanging you along the way.

And somewhere in this journey, I discovered that Dhanush means"bow".Not because it defined my path, but because I like what it represents —a bow gathers strength before moving forward.

This is not the end of my story.It is simply the next beginning.
